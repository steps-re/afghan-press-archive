سراج الاخبار افغانیه

Volume 1, number 1 — Kabul, 1911. Mahmud Tarzi's Torch of the News, printed at the Mashin Khana on a press the state had only just acquired. It ran for seven years, argued for schools, railways and a modern Afghan nation, and taught a generation of writers their trade. Every later paper in this archive descends from it.

The collection

Books and periodicals printed in Afghanistan between 1873 and the 1960s, digitised by New York University Libraries, who hold the scans. The works are in the public domain.

The transcription

Read by machine, not by hand, and not checked. On 102 pages sampled across the difficulty range, the median character error rate against a modern edition of the same text is 0.36 — part of which is editorial difference rather than misreading. Treat the text as a finding aid and read the page image before quoting.
