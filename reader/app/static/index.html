سراج الاخبار افغانیه

Volume 1, number 1 — Kabul, 1911. Mahmud Tarzi's Torch of the News, printed at the Mashin Khana on a press the state had only just acquired. It ran for seven years, argued for schools, railways and a modern Afghan nation, and taught a generation of writers their trade. Every later paper in this archive descends from it.

The collection

Books and periodicals printed in Afghanistan between 1871 and the 1930s, digitised by New York University Libraries, who hold the scans. The works are in the public domain.

The transcription

Read by machine, not by hand, and not checked. Measured against passages transcribed by a scholar, roughly 93 characters in 100 are correct. Treat the text as a finding aid and read the page image before quoting.
